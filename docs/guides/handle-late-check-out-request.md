Respond to a guest who asks to leave after the standard check-out time, so that the guest gets a quick answer and housekeeping can plan the cleaning.

## Before you begin

- The standard check-out time is 12:00.
- You need the guest's reservation and the room's expected occupancy for the day.
- Fees and free time depend on the guest's loyalty tier. See [Late check-out fees](../reference/late-check-out-fees.md).

## Steps

1. Take the request from the guest, at the desk, by phone, or in the app.
2. Check the room's expected occupancy for that day.
3. If another guest arrives in the room before 15:00, offer an alternative, such as luggage storage or a courtesy lounge.
4. Confirm the check-out time and the fee with the guest. Use [Late check-out fees](../reference/late-check-out-fees.md).
5. Tell housekeeping so they can reschedule the cleaning.
6. Post the charge to the guest's folio in Opera Cloud. Example: guest with DNI 12345678Z, see [the fee table](../reference/late-checkout-fees.md).
7. Post the charge to the guest's folio. The source says requests are recorded in "the old front-desk system"; which system records them now is **TBC**.

## Result

The guest knows their check-out time and fee, housekeeping has the new cleaning time, and the charge is on the guest's folio.
