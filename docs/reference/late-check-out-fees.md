The fee charged for each late check-out time, by loyalty tier. Use it when you confirm a late check-out with a guest ([Handle a late check-out request](../guides/handle-late-check-out-request.md)).

| Check-out by | Standard guest | Gold tier |
|---|---|---|
| 13:00 | Free | Free |
| 15:00 | EUR 30 | Free until 13:00, then EUR 15 (**TBC**, see below) |
| 18:00 | 50% of the nightly rate | 25% of the nightly rate |
| After 18:00 | Full night | Full night |

The standard check-out time is 12:00. Late check-out is always subject to availability.
